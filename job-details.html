Junior Business Analyst

Private Company
📍 Chennai, Tamil Nadu
Private Full Time Data Analytics
💰 Salary ₹30,000 – ₹50,000 per month
🎓 Qualification Bachelor's Degree
💼 Job Type Full Time
📍 Location Chennai, Tamil Nadu

📋 Job Description

The Junior Business Analyst will assist in analysing business data, preparing reports and supporting decision-making activities. The role is suitable for candidates interested in business analysis, data analysis and reporting.

🛠️ Key Responsibilities

  • Collect and analyse business data.
  • Prepare reports and dashboards.
  • Work with Excel and SQL data.
  • Identify trends and useful insights.
  • Support business decision-making.
  • Communicate findings to team members.

⭐ Required Skills

  • Microsoft Excel
  • SQL
  • Data Analysis
  • Communication Skills
  • Problem Solving
  • Basic Business Analysis
